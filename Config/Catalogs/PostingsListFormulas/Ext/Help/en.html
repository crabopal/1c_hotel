The catalog contains a list of formulas that are used in the table of break down list settings for the accounting chart of accounts

The following list of variables is supported in formulas:

  • [Amount] - amount of the main service (charge amount)
  • [DiscountAmount] - discount amount
  • [CommissionAmount] - amount of Commission
  • [Price] - price of the main service
  • [Quantity] - quantity of the main service
  • [VATRate] - VAT rate of the main service as a number
  • [VATAmount] - VAT amount of the main service
  • [AccountingDate] - accounting date of the main service
  • [NumberOfPersons] - the number of people in the reservation
  • [NumberOfAdults] - the number of adults in the reservation
  • [NumberOfTeenagers] - the number of teenagers in the reservation
  • [NumberOfChildren] - the number of children in the reservation
  • [NumberOfInfants] - the number of babies in the reservation
