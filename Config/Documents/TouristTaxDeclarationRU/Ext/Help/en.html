Document "Tourist tax declaration (RU)"

Purpose

The document generates a list of reservations for the specified period (quarter) with the amounts of tourist tax payable in the selected period. Reservations are selected by the hotel and company (legal entity of the hotel) specified in the declaration by the date of accrual of tourist tax specified in the reservation. 

Relates to the subsystem: «Accounts management».

Parameters

Number length: 12, type: String

Frequency: Year

Type: String
